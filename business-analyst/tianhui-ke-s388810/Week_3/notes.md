# Week 3 — Stakeholder Management & Delivery (Business Analyst)

## Course notes
**Business Analysis: Managing Stakeholder Expectations**
- Stakeholders are managed with a Power/Interest grid: high-power/high-interest stakeholders need active engagement, low-power/low-interest just need to be informed.
- Expectation mismatches (not lack of skill) are the most common cause of stakeholder dissatisfaction — regular, honest status updates prevent this.

**Agile Product Owner: Managing the Product Backlog**
- The backlog is a single, ordered list of everything the product might need, owned by the Product Owner and continuously re-prioritised.
- Items at the top of the backlog should be small and well-refined (ready to build); items further down can stay rough (epics) until they get closer to being worked on.

**Jira Essential Training**
- A Jira board visualises backlog items moving through workflow states (To Do → In Progress → Done) during a sprint.
- Epics group related stories; story points estimate relative effort, used to plan how much a team can commit to per sprint.
