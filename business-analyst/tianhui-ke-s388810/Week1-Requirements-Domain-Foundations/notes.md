# Week 1 — Requirements & Domain Foundations (Business Analyst)

## Course notes
**Business Analysis Foundations**
- BA role sits between stakeholders and delivery teams; core job is turning a business problem into clear, testable requirements.
- Key deliverables: Business Requirements Document (BRD), user stories, process models, and a requirements traceability matrix.
- Requirements are elicited (interviews, workshops, observation), then analysed, documented, validated with stakeholders, and managed through change.

**Business Analysis: Gathering and Documenting Requirements**
- Elicitation techniques: interviews, workshops, surveys, document analysis, observation — pick based on stakeholder availability and complexity.
- Requirements should be categorised as Business, Stakeholder, Solution (Functional/Non-functional), and Transition requirements.
- Good requirements are unambiguous, testable, and traceable back to a business need — vague requirements cause scope creep later.

**Agile Requirements Foundations**
- In Agile, requirements are expressed as user stories ("As a [role], I want [goal], so that [benefit]") instead of long specs.
- Each story needs acceptance criteria (Given/When/Then) so the team and QA agree on "done".
- The backlog is a living document — stories are refined and re-prioritised every sprint rather than frozen upfront like in Waterfall BRDs.
