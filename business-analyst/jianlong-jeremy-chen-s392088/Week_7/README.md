# Week 1 Study Materials

**Topics:** Business Analysis / Agile Product Ownership / Jira
**Mapped to:**
1. Business Analysis: Managing Stakeholder Expectations
2. Agile Product Owner: Managing the Product Backlog
3. Jira Essential Training
4. Practice task: build a mock backlog for your Week 1 feature in a free Jira board

> ⚠️ Note: The three courses above are paid LinkedIn Learning courses. I don't have access to their internal video footage or slide screenshots (copyright + no account access). What follows are original notes covering the **same body of knowledge** (concepts, methods, and diagrams are all created from scratch), meant for preview/review. At the end of the README you'll find **free, shareable videos** on the same topics that you can pass along to colleagues or use as a reference when recording your assignment.

## Suggested study path (~3-4 hours)

| Order | Content | Est. time | Output |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Read `01-stakeholder-management.md`, understand stakeholder analysis methods | 40 min | A power/interest grid sketch |
| 2 | Read `02-agile-product-backlog.md`, understand epics/stories/prioritization | 50 min | A sample backlog |
| 3 | Read `03-jira-essentials.md`, follow along in free Jira | 60 min | A working Jira project |
| 4 | Complete the practice task per `04-practice-mock-sprint.md` | 60-90 min | Jira board + 15-min mock sprint planning recording |
